Nucleus

Solid core of ice, dust and rock. Typically 1-10km.

Coma

Cloud of gas and dust sublimating off the nucleus, up to 1M km wide.

Ion Tail

Plasma blown directly away from the Sun by solar wind. Always points anti-sunward.

Dust Tail

Curved trail of dust particles following the orbital path. Reflects sunlight.
